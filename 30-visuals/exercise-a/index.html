About the Course

The Master degree in Public and Corporate Communication provides advanced knowledge in communication strategies, digital media, and public relations. It prepares students for international careers in corporate communication, marketing agencies, and public institutions.

Study Plan

Our multidisciplinary curriculum combines theory and practice across key areas:

  • Corporate Communication & Branding
  • Digital Marketing & Social Media Strategies
  • Public Relations & Crisis Management
  • Media Studies & Communication Research

Admission & Location

Classes are held in the historic center of Milan. Non-EU and EU students can apply online through the official portal. The campus of PUBLIC AND CORPORATE COMMUNICATION located on Via Conservatoria 7. Click on the video to watch the video.
